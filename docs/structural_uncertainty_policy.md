# Structural uncertainty policy

## Purpose

The deterministic updated model is not fitted to the spread among Young et al.
(2014), Liu et al. (2021), Cao and Bao (2013), or Luz et al. (1999). Their
differences identify response mechanisms and domains where architecture matters.
They do not constitute repeated observations of model error and therefore do
not define a Gaussian likelihood standard deviation.

The frozen classification is
`model_data/literature/multimodel_evidence_matrix_v1.json`.
Its human-readable summary is `docs/multimodel_evidence_matrix.md`.

## Three uncertainty layers

1. Measurement uncertainty is reported in the measured isotope coordinate and
   may enter the likelihood directly.
2. Parameter uncertainty is propagated only from declared literature ranges or
   explicit priors. It is not inferred from mismatch with a desired curve.
3. Model discrepancy is estimated only for a declared application domain with
   out-of-sample observations and fixed-input assumptions. Elsewhere,
   inter-model disagreement remains a non-probabilistic structural sensitivity
   envelope.

The current evidence does not justify one public default model-discrepancy
sigma. The Yang-Banerjee record can support a low-pCO2, 1 PAL, fixed-GPP
predictive residual after blocked validation. The Brandon event can support an
event-specific residual. Neither may be extrapolated across the full
50-60,000 ppm, 0.1-2 PAL, 5-232% GPP model domain.

The raw Yang audit now provides that domain-limited estimate. Eight
age-contiguous holdout blocks give a 14.28 ppm isotope-coordinate predictive
RMSE. For the 249 rows with positive reported analytical uncertainties, a
heteroscedastic decomposition retains isotope and propagated CO2 measurement
variance and estimates 13.24 ppm (0.01324 per mil) of excess predictive
scatter. This quantity includes unmodeled GPP/pO2 variability, chronology and
reference-coordinate effects as well as possible model error. It is therefore
registered as an optional late-Quaternary candidate, not promoted to the public
default likelihood.

The machine-readable implementation is
`model_data/uncertainty/updated_o2_uncertainty_layers_v1.json`. The pointwise
API is `code/updated_uncertainty_layers.py`. It returns measurement,
source-isoflux parameter, biological parameter, numerical, and structural
components as separate objects. It intentionally has no combined public
confidence interval.

The reproducible domain audit is generated by
`validation/audit_uncertainty_layers.py`. At 1 PAL O2, its 50% and 100%
modern-GPP transects show three distinct regimes:

- near modern pCO2, the biological-corner and source-isoflux parameter
  sensitivities are larger than the tested structural offset;
- from approximately 1,000 to 10,000 ppm, the Adnew source-isoflux endpoints
  are the largest bounded parameter sensitivity;
- at 30,000-60,000 ppm, the fixed-temperature versus Clima atmospheric
  architecture difference is several per mil and dominates all bounded
  parameter and numerical terms.

The Clima structural envelope now contains two named pressure conventions:
additive CO2 and fixed total dry major gas. Their global O2 predictions differ
by at most 0.0404 per mil through 30,000 ppm and 0.2270 per mil over the full
tested domain, much less than the several-per-mil fixed-profile versus Clima
shift. Both remain non-probabilistic end members; neither is promoted to the
central model.

The structural contract also carries exact, non-interpolated Clima end members
at 0.1 and 2 PAL O2. The pO2 cross shows a maximum high-pCO2 global O2
Delta-prime-17O shift of 9.336 per mil at 0.1 PAL, 5.528 per mil at 1 PAL and
4.916 per mil at 2 PAL. These values demonstrate a material pO2-climate-ozone
interaction; they remain scenario alternatives and are not combined into a
probability distribution.

This transition is evidence for domain-dependent structural uncertainty. It
is not evidence for a Gaussian error distribution and is not applied as a
high-pCO2 damping term.

## High-pCO2 and low-GPP use

The Liu and Cao-Bao comparisons agree with the updated model on the principal
response direction but disagree in amplitude toward high pCO2 and low GPP.
That spread is reported as architecture sensitivity rather than statistical
confidence. The physical-domain mask remains a hard mass-balance condition.

The mechanism audit decomposes the disagreement into:

- atmosphere-accessible O2 turnover versus total GPP;
- pO2-dependent photochemical source strength and vertical access;
- air-sea gas-transfer and respiratory recycling assumptions;
- atmospheric O2 inventory and residence-time conventions;
- high-pCO2 photochemical saturation and source-positivity behavior.

The source-backed marine-accessibility experiment is now complete. It improves
the Liu shared-grid response RMSE by 0.124 per mil but worsens Young Fig. 8 and
the Yang low-CO2 tracking guardrails. It is therefore retained as a
non-probabilistic biological-boundary sensitivity and is not inserted into the
central total-global-GPP calculation.

A term may be promoted into the updated model only when its equations and
parameters are traceable to independent literature, mass and isotope balances
close, more than one validation family improves, and the term is not an output
correction. The original implementation remains reproducible so the effect of
the promoted mechanism can be shown explicitly.

## Posterior use

The joint posterior engine already requires provenance for any nonzero Gaussian
model-discrepancy term. Until a domain-specific empirical estimate passes its
own validation gate, the public default remains unset. Conditional posterior
ridges may still propagate analytical and declared parameter uncertainty; their
scope must state that whole-domain structural discrepancy is not calibrated.
